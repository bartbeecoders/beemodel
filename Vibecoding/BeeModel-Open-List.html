Open features & issues

Everything that is open, in one place: the plan stages still to build, known bugs, performance follow-ups, features that were put off, and loose ends on GitHub. The plans say what to build. This list says what is still open. It is updated with every commit that opens or closes something here.

Now

What is being worked on, and what comes next.

In progress: Stage T · Text → v1.2.0

Milestone 26, issues #387–#391. Branch text. 3D text from TrueType fonts, animated letter by letter, and text the AI can add and change. See the 2.0 plan. Stage Q (Ask AI) is released as v1.1.0 (2026-10-23).

In progress: Stage C · Render to video → v1.3.0

Milestone 27, issues #394–#397. Branch video (on text). Render › Render Animation… into .mp4 / .webm / .gif through ffmpeg or an animated PNG without it, with progress and Esc. See the 2.0 plan.

Next: Stage V · Smoke, fire, volumes → v1.4.0

V1–V5 in the 2.0 plan: a sparse gas grid, fire, volumes in both path tracers and in the viewport, caching and OpenVDB export. After that, stage Z (v2.0.0).

Plan stages left

The 2.0 plan: R, G, S, A and P are released (v0.17–v0.22), as are two stages that were added along the way: rendering performance H (v0.23) and the GPU viewport VP (v0.24). The decisions D are released as v1.0.0.
