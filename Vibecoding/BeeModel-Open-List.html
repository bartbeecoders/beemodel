Open features & issues

Everything that is open, in one place: the plan stages still to build, known bugs, performance follow-ups, features that were put off, and loose ends on GitHub. The plans say what to build. This list says what is still open. It is updated with every commit that opens or closes something here.

Now

What is being worked on, and what comes next.

Released: v1.0.0 · Stage D · Decisions

Milestone 17, issues #293–#299, merged in #372 and tagged 2026-10-22. From here on, .bee files, BeeScript and the MCP tools change only by adding (docs/DECISIONS.md).

Next: Stage V · Smoke, fire, volumes

V1–V5 in the 2.0 plan: a sparse gas grid, fire, volumes in both path tracers and in the viewport, caching and OpenVDB export. After that, stage Z (v2.0.0).
